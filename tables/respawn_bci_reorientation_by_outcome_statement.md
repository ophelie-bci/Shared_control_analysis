# Respawn Decoded BCI Reorientation by Outcome

Post-jump decoded BCI-velocity reorientation latency was computed from `modelVelocity` for both shared-control and BCI-only Respawn trials.
Latency was defined as the first post-jump time at which old-vs-new target evidence remained positive for at least 200 ms.

- AI correct: n=535, crossed=99.8%, median latency=384.3 ms.
- AI incorrect: n=260, crossed=75.4%, median latency=683.0 ms.
- No AI correct: n=635, crossed=100.0%, median latency=449.8 ms.
- No AI incorrect: n=165, crossed=73.3%, median latency=543.0 ms.

- AI: incorrect-minus-correct median latency difference = 298.7 ms; Mann-Whitney p=1.95e-21.
- No AI: incorrect-minus-correct median latency difference = 93.2 ms; Mann-Whitney p=0.00975.
