# Collision-corrected Success Counterfactual

Counterfactual: every BCI-only failed trial that contacted the obstacle is counted as a success, with all other outcomes unchanged.
Rows source: raw_sessions.

- Appearing Obstacle: across 28 sessions, mean R_s = 8.6 pp and median R_s = 10.1 pp after collision correction. Two-sided Wilcoxon against zero: p = 3.64e-04; directional R_s > 0 p = 1.82e-04. 21/28 sessions had R_s > 0. Mean collision-removal term = 19.5 pp of the 28.1 pp observed improvement (82.2%).
- Fixed Obstacle: across 23 sessions, mean R_s = 23.8 pp and median R_s = 22.0 pp after collision correction. Two-sided Wilcoxon against zero: p = 2.38e-07; directional R_s > 0 p = 1.19e-07. 23/23 sessions had R_s > 0. Mean collision-removal term = 5.7 pp of the 29.5 pp observed improvement (20.4%).

Collision definition: minimum avatar-obstacle distance <= 0.70 m.
